What the system is learning

1 · Explore physical shots

Vary board center height, release gap, board tilt, board-to-bucket distance and bearing around the bucket. The board faces the bucket and the dropper releases above it. Ballistics helps propose configurations; IK and actual simulation determine feasibility and scoring.

2 · Train a shared ranking policy

A shared policy sees bucket geometry and each qualified candidate’s shot features. Categorical PPO learns which configurations to choose, with uniform initial exploration. Invalid attempts and misses remain in the record; successful examples do not remove the failures.

3 · Test without further search

Freeze the initial and final checkpoints and run fresh episodes. A capture requires a board bounce, whole-ball entry and one continuous second inside, without a table, floor or robot-assisted route. New bucket layouts need a separate disjoint test before any generalization claim.

Simulation uses privileged geometry, provisional contact properties, scripted endpoint setup and initialized releases. This is shot selection, not learned grasping or joint-path control. Cameras and point clouds do not drive this policy.
